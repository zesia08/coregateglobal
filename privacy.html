Coregate Global ("CGglobal", "we", "our", or "us") operates Shopping Short-form Maker. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when users connect social media accounts and use the service.

Depending on the permissions selected by the user and the connected platform, we may process:

  • authorization tokens and related technical information required to provide the requested connection;
  • content and metadata submitted by the user for creation, editing, management, or upload; and

3. Purpose of Processing

We process information only as reasonably necessary to:

4. TikTok Account Information

When a user connects a TikTok account through TikTok OAuth, the service may process only the information permitted by the user's authorization and the scopes approved by TikTok.

We do not collect or store TikTok account passwords.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain security, resolve issues, or comply with applicable legal obligations. 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by connected social media platforms when users choose to connect or use those platforms. We may also use service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate the service and subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. User Choices and Rights

Users may request access, correction, deletion, or disconnection of linked account information by contacting us. Users may also revoke permissions through the settings provided by the relevant social media platform.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

9. Children's Privacy

The service is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use the connected platforms or to provide the required consent under applicable law.
